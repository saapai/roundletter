# Collect 200 — "everything will be alright"

**Source:** Album/poster art — COLLECT 200
**Saved:** 2026-05-18

## Visual Description

Two silhouetted figures walking a tightrope against a deep blue sky, a vivid rainbow arcs behind them. The composition is diagonal (tightrope runs bottom-left to upper-right). Dark vignette frames the image with rounded corners.

## What works

- **Rainbow on deep blue** — the rainbow reads as hopeful/transcendent against the moody sky
- **Silhouette simplicity** — figures are pure black, no detail, all gesture
- **Diagonal tension** — the tightrope creates the same diagonal energy as the archive light shaft
- **Typography restraint** — "COLLECT 200" top-left in bold sans, "everything will be alright" top-right in light weight. Both white. No other text.
- **The contrast**: pure joy/optimism (rainbow, title) against precariousness (tightrope, silhouettes)
- **Rounded corners + dark vignette** — feels like a Polaroid or vinyl sleeve

## Portable to roundletter

- **Rainbow on dark** — the "call me if you get lost" / "aureliex." closer could use true rainbow colors (not muted rose-gold) if placed on a dark background
- **The tightrope diagonal** — same energy as the archive light shaft, could inform section dividers
- **Optimism-through-precariousness** — thematic match for the $3,453 → $100,000 bet
- **The phrase** "everything will be alright" — same energy as "drinks are on me"
